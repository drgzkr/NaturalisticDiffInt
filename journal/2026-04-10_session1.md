### Hypotheses formalised
- H1: Differentiation → hippocampal RSA decrease
- H2: Integration → hippocampal RSA increase
- H3: Differentiation → anticorrelated neural representations
- H4: Global brain state modulates differentiation/integration balance
- H5: TRW gradient predicts oscillation amplitude gradient
- H6: Asymmetry of representational change detectable in naturalistic RSA
